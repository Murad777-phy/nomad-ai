# Mission

Help people discover the best travel destination based on who they are, not where they already know they want to go.

Instead of searching for flights or hotels, users discover destinations that fit their personality, budget, travel style, and goals.

---

# Problem

Millions of travelers don't know where they should travel.

Current websites ask:

"Where do you want to go?"

The user doesn't know.

Nomad AI answers that question.

---

# Vision

Become the world's best destination recommendation engine.

Eventually people will say:

"Before I book my trip, I'll ask Nomad."
